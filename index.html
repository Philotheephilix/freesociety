The permission layer

Your agent spends.
It never holds the keys.

Errand gives the agent a scoped spending mandate — not your wallet. You cap exactly how much it can spend, and on which modes. Anything outside the mandate is simply not authorized.

How it works

Four steps. Zero clicks from you.

Step 01

Scope the mandate

Connect your wallet via OAuth and set a spend mandate — modes and a daily cap. Keys never leave your custody.

Step 02

Tell your agent

Say what to buy. One instruction — the agent plans the rest and runs it autonomously.

Step 03

Agent pays in crypto

It spends within its permission limits — x402 metered calls or a scoped contract-mode purchase.

Step 04

Gap-filling

If the purchase needs an app with no API, a cloud-emulated phone completes it automatically.
