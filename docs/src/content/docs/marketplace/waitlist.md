The waitlist is the front door to the [integration
marketplace](/marketplace/overview/). It is where a company registers interest
before it has a fully-specified [request](/marketplace/request/). The
marketplace manages the waitlist: you apply, we review, and we help you turn a
rough idea into a request our developers can build.

## Who the waitlist is for

- Companies that know they want an agent to automate a job, but have not yet
  pinned down the exact actions an integration needs.
- Teams evaluating whether Errand can close a specific gap — the way it closed
  the Swiggy Money redemption gap.
- Anyone who wants early access as integrations roll out.

## What to send

A short application is enough to get on the list:

```md
## Company
<name and a one-line description>

## What you want automated
<the job, in plain language>

## The service involved
<the platform or API, if you know it>

## Volume
<rough transaction volume you expect — helps us prioritize>

## Contact
<how we reach you>
```

You do not need the full [request format](/marketplace/request/) yet — that
comes after we talk.

## What happens next

1. **Apply.** Send the short application above.
2. **Review.** We assess fit and reach out.
3. **Shape.** We help you turn the idea into a complete pull-request-style
   [request](/marketplace/request/).
4. **Build and ship.** Our developers implement it; the tools appear on the hub.

## The terms stay the same

Joining the waitlist commits you to nothing and costs nothing. When an
integration ships, the only charge is the standard **1% of Bitrefill's
commission** on settled transactions — see the
[Business model](/marketplace/business-model/).

We provide MCP services for any company that wants its job automated. The
waitlist is where that starts.
